## What's true in every variation (the fixed points)

**The rebrand.** MAJOR TV → **MAJOR CONTENT**. We already own clean traced SVG
paths of the MAJOR wordmark (from the previous build, in `archive/public/brand/`),
so the new lockup is straightforward: "CONTENT" set in the same thin tracked caps
as the BOOKS / BRAND DEVELOPMENT descriptor lines, centered edge-to-edge under
MAJOR per RG's instruction. One production note: no letter **C** exists in any
current descriptor lockup, so "CONTENT" gets set in the matched face rather than
assembled from existing art — invisible to anyone but us.

**The intro animation.** RG's copy doc scripts it precisely, and the Uncontained
recording shows the pacing: full-screen lines fade in and out in sequence —
*"Bridging Content and Culture / with / Global Icons / Premium Programming /
Worldwide Bestsellers / Resonating with the collective majority / That's /
MAJOR CONTENT / TV | FILM | BOOKS"* — resolving into a slowed version of the
MAJOR logo build (wings → M → letters spread → descriptor), then **holding** on
the lockup. It plays once, no loop.

Key lesson from the Uncontained teardown: they baked their intro into a 1080p
MP4 — heavy, blurry at odd sizes, invisible to Google, unskippable. **We build
it as live text + the animated SVG wordmark instead**: crisp at every size, a
few KB, real indexable copy, skippable on scroll, and it degrades gracefully for
reduced-motion users. (We don't need the raw After Effects file to do this, but
having it would let us match the metallic shading of the M exactly.)

**The projects wall.** All 24 tiles from the copy doc — shows and books
interleaved in ONE grid, exactly as RG framed it: *"impressive as a sum."* Every
tile carries its proof line (Emmy Award-Winning · Hulu / #1 New York Times
Bestseller / International Bestseller) using the badge art he sent. The four
show tiles link to their trailers; books stay static. No case pages, no
per-project deep-dives — the wall itself is the argument.

**The story.** Publisher & producer under one roof; culturally inspired, not
culturally exclusive. TV | FILM | BOOKS.

**Also true everywhere:** majorcontent.com, mobile-first, fast (the previous
build's 95–100 Lighthouse discipline carries over), and the old
majortvproductions.com 301-redirects to the new domain.

## Variation 1 — "THE REEL"
### RadicalMedia DNA — the work is the hero

**Thesis:** No headline. No manifesto above the fold. A fast-cut montage of
DRIVE, TIME OF ESSENCE, REMIX MY SPACE, and Vegas Residency footage intercut
with book covers snapping past — and the MAJOR CONTENT logo animation plays
*over* it. The credits do the talking.

**The first ten seconds.** Full-bleed muted montage loop (poster frame renders
instantly, video crossfades in when ready — Radical's exact mechanic). The
word-flow copy plays as white type over the footage, ends on the lockup, then
the type quiets and the reel keeps breathing behind it. This is the closest
match to RG's ask: *"trailers and book images quickly flash behind animation."*

**Page structure (single scroll):**
1. Montage hero + word-flow/logo animation overlay
2. One-line positioning ("Bridging Content and Culture") — small, then out of the way
3. **The wall**: mixed masonry — 16:9 show tiles (2–3 of them live muted trailer
   loops, capped for performance) among 2:3 book covers. The aspect-ratio
   contrast itself says "screen + page" without a word of copy
4. Tight caption grammar on every tile: *Title / Format / proof line* — with
   platform bugs (Hulu, OWN, HBO Max, discovery+, VH1) on shows and bestseller
   badges on books, so credibility lands at scan speed
5. Company (the two paragraphs), Founder, Contact — compact, at the bottom

**Look:** Strict monochrome chrome — black/white/gray only, one heavy grotesque.
Zero decorative color, so the key art and jackets supply ALL the color. Feels
like a cutting-room wall.

**Why pick it:** It's RG's stated favorite reference, executed for his catalog.
Most confident posture: the company that doesn't need to explain itself.
**Trade-off:** Demands a well-cut 20–40s montage reel (an editing task, not just
a web task) and trailer footage rights for that cut. The books read as guests in
a TV company's house unless the cover beats in the reel are given real weight.

## Variation 2 — "THE MARQUEE"
### Fifth Season DNA — a slate company with two shelves

**Thesis:** MAJOR CONTENT presented the way a premium studio presents itself: a
full-screen rotating marquee of key art where an Emmy-winning show is followed
by a #1 NYT bestseller *as equals* — same frame, same treatment, alternating
screen and page.

**The first ten seconds.** The word-flow intro plays on black, resolves to the
lockup — then the lockup shrinks to the header as the marquee fades up behind
it: full-viewport art rotating on a timed progress bar (DRIVE → TRUE LOVE →
TIME OF ESSENCE → THE LOVE PLAYBOOK...), each slide carrying only the title and
its proof line.

**Page structure:**
1. Intro → full-screen mixed marquee with progress bar
2. Company statement over a quiet gradient
3. **Three rails**: TV | FILM · BOOKS · and a "Together" rail of the four joint
   credits with trailer links — all slices of one content model, so an
   adaptation can appear on both shelves and cross-link between its forms
4. Full wall (all 24) behind a "View the slate" moment
5. Founder (his four-act arc: REG → Rayo → Celebra → MAJOR), Contact

**Look:** Dark cinematic base with one warm accent ramp — one end of the ramp
belongs to *screen*, the other to *page*, used as hairline borders and hover
washes so the two divisions stay distinct but unmistakably one company. A
single modern face with real range.

**Why pick it:** The most "big studio" of the three — it frames MAJOR next to
Fifth Season rather than next to boutiques, and it's the best home for a growing
slate (coming-soon tiles for unannounced projects slot right in; the structure
scales for years). **Trade-off:** The most built of the three (carousel system,
rails, per-vertical pages). Its scale flatters a deep slate — with 4 shows + 20
books it must be art-directed carefully so it doesn't feel like a jacket
stretched over a smaller frame.

## Variation 3 — "THE MANIFESTO"
### Uncontained lineage × Transient discipline — one page, one argument

**Thesis:** The word-flow intro isn't a preroll before the site — it *is* the
site. One black, cinematic page that reads top to bottom like the argument RG
wrote: culture → content → proof → founder. His copy doc already has exactly
this structure; this variation builds it verbatim.

**The first ten seconds.** Identical DNA to the Uncontained intro he loves, but
rebuilt properly: live type, patient pacing, lines breathing in and out on
black, resolving to the slowed logo build. Then — the difference — scrolling
*continues the sentence*: each section arrives with the same word-flow language,
so the intro's voice never breaks.

**Page structure (one page, six beats):**
1. Word-flow intro → lockup holds
2. COMPANY — the two paragraphs, large serif-quiet type on black
3. TV | FILM and BOOKS — two facing statements (the two rooms of one house)
4. **The wall** — but staged as a slow perpetual drift: one continuous marquee
   strip of all 24 tiles at a uniform poster ratio, shows and books shoulder to
   shoulder (the single best idea from the old Uncontained site, done right:
   every tile badged, four of them opening trailers). A "credential moment"
   punctuates it — one full-screen beat: *"Emmy Award-Winning. New York Times
   Bestselling. Same roof."*
5. FOUNDER — Transient-style: not a résumé timeline but a told story, ending on
   the People en Español recognition as a pull-quote
6. Contact — one email, one line

**Look:** Black-on-black cinema, sparse, generous silence between beats. Type
does almost all the work; motion is slow and editorial (drift, fade, settle —
nothing bounces).

**Why pick it:** Purest expression of RG's own copy and the direct heir to the
Uncontained intro he asked for — and since uncontainedmedia.com is now offline
(the domain currently doesn't resolve), this look is unclaimed territory he can
own outright. Fastest to launch: no CMS, no case pages, one page done
exquisitely. **Trade-off:** One page means one story — no per-project depth, no
rails to grow into. If the slate doubles, this becomes the elegant brochure that
precedes Variation 2.

intro + Variation 1's wall now**, and grow into Variation 2's structure as the
slate expands. Worth putting in front of RG as an option.
