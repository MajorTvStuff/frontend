# Book & show write-ups — for RG's sign-off

_Compiled 18 September 2026. **Nothing here is on the site yet.**_

Every summary below is **written by us** from published sources — publisher listings, trade press,
network announcements — and put in our own words; no jacket copy is copied. The facts underneath each
one (publisher, imprint, year, network, co-writers) are cited, and the links are in
`site/content/research.json` next to each entry.

**Raymond's role is not guessed anywhere.** It comes from one of two places:

- **his own bio** (the copy doc he already approved) — which names all four shows as Executive Producer,
  and names LORD HELP ME as literary representation; or
- **the imprint**, which is a matter of public record — the book was published by Celebra or by Rayo,
  and his bio states he founded and published the first and was Associate Publisher of the second.

One title (CELIA, MY LIFE) is the single case where the connection is not spelled out either way. It is
flagged below.

**What we need from you:** read the summaries, change any wording you want changed, and say yes. Anything
marked ❓ is a question only you can answer.

## Shows

### DRIVE — with Swizz Beatz

Swizz Beatz and his eldest son, Nasir Dean, travel to Los Angeles, New York, Houston, Atlanta, Japan and Saudi Arabia to meet the people who build, race and collect cars, and to bring separate car communities together around the same obsession.

- **Released:** Hulu, for Onyx Collective — six episodes, premiered 16 November 2023
- **Award:** Won the 2024 Daytime Emmy for Outstanding Single Camera Editing; also nominated for Outstanding Sound Mixing and Sound Editing
- **Raymond's role:** Executive Producer  
  _(source: his own bio)_
- **On the site now as:** “Emmy Award-Winning · Hulu”
- ❓ **Does he want the Emmy named by category ('Outstanding Single Camera Editing, 2024 Daytime Emmy Awards')? This is the open question 6 in the concepts doc, now answerable.**

### TIME OF ESSENCE

Five hours on fifty years of Essence, a decade to an episode — how the magazine reported and shaped Black American culture, told by the people who were in it. With Oprah Winfrey, Halle Berry, Whoopi Goldberg, Taraji P. Henson, Regina Hall, Taye Diggs, Gabrielle Union and Beverly Johnson.

- **Released:** OWN — five parts, premiered 18 August 2023; streams on Max
- **Raymond's role:** Executive Producer  
  _(source: his own bio)_
- **On the site now as:** “OWN / HBO Max”

### REMIX MY SPACE — with Marsai Martin

Marsai Martin and a design team surprise teenagers who have done something for their community, and rebuild their bedrooms around what each of them actually cares about — photography, robotics, sneakers.

- **Released:** Discovery+ — eight episodes, premiered January 2022; also on HBO Max
- **Raymond's role:** Executive Producer  
  _(source: his own bio)_
- **On the site now as:** “Discovery+ / HBO Max”

### BEHIND THE VEGAS RESIDENCY — Ricky Martin

A backstage hour on Ricky Martin's 'All In' residency at the Park Theater in Las Vegas — rehearsals, career flashbacks and the days before opening night.

- **Released:** VH1 — one-hour special, aired 4 September 2017
- **Raymond's role:** Executive Producer  
  _(source: his own bio)_
- **On the site now as:** “VH1”

## Books

### TRUE LOVE — Jennifer Lopez
