# Archive

Nothing in here runs. Kept for reference and for the work that was carried
forward into `../site/`.

**These are the superseded variants.** The two that are still live — the
copy-led page at `/` and the image-led one at `/image-led` — are both in
`../site/`, and are being shown against each other; see the root README.
Whichever loses joins this folder.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `majortv-rebuild/` | The finished Next.js rebuild of **majortvproductions.com**, from before the rebrand. `legacy/` inside it is the scraped 2018 original and is the source of truth for that site — the measured palette, the traced wordmark and the CLS/perf techniques used in `site/` all came from here. |
| `manifesto/` | Concept 03 of the rebrand taste test: one black cinematic page, the word-flow intro as the whole site. Ran on port 3006 from the repository root, which is why the root looked like an app. |
| `reel-v1/` | The first build of the rebrand — "the Reel", promoted to beta MVP, then replaced. Its intro choreography, wordmark tracing, play-once trailer contract and player-bus coordination were all carried forward. |
| `notes/` | `CONCEPTS.html`, the double-click launcher from the taste-test period, and `REEL-PASS-3-PLAN.md`, the build plan for a version that no longer exists. |

Why `reel-v1` was replaced, from the audit that judged it: *"the page performs
beautifully and argues nothing."* A cold visitor scrolled 2.6 screens before
the site said what the company was, and the client's one differentiating claim
sat at 15px, 81% of the way down the page.
